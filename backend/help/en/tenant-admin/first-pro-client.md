# Your first client in TrackPal Pro

Follow this order to finish setup without going back and forth.

## 1. Prepare the Catalog

Create the service you offer and add at least one plan.

## 2. Create the client

Enter their name, username, password, and optional phone. Save and give them the full username generated by TrackPal.

## 3. Create the subscription

Open **Subscriptions** from the client, choose the prepared service and plan, add the service email, and set the duration.

In WhatsApp, **Clients**, **Catalog**, and **Subscriptions** are available in the **TrackPal Pro** menu. Use `9` to go back and `0` to cancel.

If the final step fails, the Catalog and client remain available. Review the highlighted value and create the subscription again.
