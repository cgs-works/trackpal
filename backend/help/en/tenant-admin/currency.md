# Currency

This setting is available on TrackPal Pro.

Open **My Account > Regional settings** and choose the currency that best represents your business. The official currency of the selected country appears first above a separator in the currency picker.

The currency symbol comes from the bundled catalog (for example, "Bs." for Venezuela). Plan prices throughout TrackPal are displayed with this symbol. If a plan has no price set, the catalog shows "Price on request."

You can set a plan price from the web catalog editor or from the WhatsApp console.
