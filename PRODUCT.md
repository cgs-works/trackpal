# Product

## Register
product

## Users
- **Master operators**: Manage the platform, tenant lifecycle, and system health.
- **Tenant operators**: Manage their own WhatsApp service delivery, service catalogs, subscription plans, and clients.
- **Clients**: End-users who subscribe to tenant services and access a read-only profile dashboard.

## Product Purpose
Trackpal is a multi-tenant SaaS for managing WhatsApp-based service delivery. It allows businesses (tenants) to manage their clients and subscriptions via web dashboard and WhatsApp bot interfaces. Success means operators can work quickly, confidently, and efficiently without interface friction.

## Brand Personality
Professional, clean, and functional. The interface should feel reliable and fast, akin to modern high-craft tools like Linear, Stripe, or Notion. Minimalist foundation with thoughtful structural constraints and a clear accent color.

## Anti-references
- Classic dense corporate software (bloated, slow, overwhelming).
- Empty excessive minimalism (too much white space at the cost of information density).
- Excessive visual decoration (no glassmorphism, unnecessary shadows, or decorative gradients).

## Design Principles
- **Expert Confidence**: The tool should feel snappy, reliable, and get out of the way of the operator's workflow.
- **Dense but Clear**: Balance information density with crisp visual hierarchy so data is easy to scan.
- **Actions Speak**: Primary actions should be unmistakable. Avoid burying important flows in ambiguous navigation.
- **Function Over Form**: Every visual element must serve a purpose. Remove purely decorative treatments.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Standard standard baseline. Ensure sufficient contrast (no light gray on white) and basic keyboard navigation for power users. No strict WCAG AA/AAA compliance required, but it should feel accessible and legible.
